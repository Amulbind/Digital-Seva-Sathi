Four portals every citizen should know

These are the services people ask about most. Tap "Steps" on any card to see the walkthrough, or go straight to the official site.

AAD
✓ Aadhaar Services
AAD · 01

Aadhaar Services

Use myAadhaar to download e-Aadhaar and access supported online services. Mobile-number updates and biometric services require an authorised Aadhaar centre visit.

View the steps
  1. 1Go to myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in and click "Login" — Aadhaar doesn't use a separate signup, your Aadhaar number is your ID.
  2. 2Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number (or Virtual ID) and the captcha shown.
  3. 3Click "Send OTP" and enter the OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number.
  4. 4From the dashboard, download e-Aadhaar or use the online services currently offered. Mobile-number updates and biometric work require an authorised Aadhaar centre visit.
  5. 5Never enrolled before, or lost your mobile link? Visit your nearest Aadhaar Seva Kendra — enrolment itself needs an in-person biometric visit.

PAN
✓ PAN Card
PAN · 02

PAN Card

Eligible first-time applicants can use Instant e-PAN on the Income Tax portal. Other PAN applications, corrections, or physical cards use authorised provider services with fees shown there.

View the steps
  1. 1Go to incometax.gov.in and find "Instant e-PAN" under Quick Links (needs Aadhaar linked to an active mobile number).
  2. 2Click "Get New e-PAN", enter your Aadhaar number, and accept the terms.
  3. 3Verify the OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile and confirm your e-KYC details.
  4. 4Keep the acknowledgement number and use the portal's Check Status / Download PAN option when the result is available.
  5. 5For a physical card, correction, or an ineligible case, follow the Income Tax portal's links to Protean or UTIITSL and check the current fee there.

DigiLocker
✓ DigiLocker
DGL · 03

DigiLocker

Create an account and fetch participating government-issued documents into your digital wallet. Issuer-fetched credentials are verifiable; uploaded scans are not issuer-issued.

View the steps
  1. 1Visit digilocker.gov.in and click "Sign Up".
  2. 2Enter your mobile number, set a 6-digit security PIN, and verify with the OTP.
  3. 3Link Aadhaar only when the account flow or the specific issuer document requires identity verification.
  4. 4Go to "Issued Documents", search the issuing department (e.g. UIDAI, CBSE, your RTO), and select the document.
  5. 5After the issuer processes the request, check Issued Documents and use the official share/download option, including QR verification if provided.

PM-JAY
✓ Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)
AYU · 04

Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)

Check eligibility and complete supported Ayushman Card steps through the NHA portal. Coverage and card access depend on scheme and state rules.

View the steps
  1. 1Go to beneficiary.nha.gov.in and choose "Beneficiary" login.
  2. 2Enter your mobile number and captcha, then verify with the OTP.
  3. 3Pick your state and scheme, then search using your Aadhaar, ration card, or family ID to check eligibility.
  4. 4If eligible, complete e-KYC (Aadhaar OTP, or fingerprint at a nearby CSC/hospital) to confirm your identity.
  5. 5Download the Ayushman Card if it becomes available. If you need assistance, contact official scheme support or an empanelled hospital; availability depends on the beneficiary record and local process.

Why this site exists

Millions of citizens are entitled to services that now live only online — but the portals aren't always easy to navigate, and language is often a barrier. This is a student-built, independent awareness project that walks through the most-used government portals in plain steps, in English, Hindi, and Marathi.

We never ask for your Aadhaar, PAN, OTP, or any personal document — every "Official portal" button takes you to the real government website to complete the process yourself, safely.
