✓ Aadhaar Services
AAD · 01

Aadhaar Services

Log in to the myAadhaar portal with your Aadhaar number and OTP to download e-Aadhaar, update your details, or order a PVC card.

View the steps
  1. 1Go to myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in and click "Login" — Aadhaar doesn't use a separate signup, your Aadhaar number is your ID.
  2. 2Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number (or Virtual ID) and the captcha shown.
  3. 3Click "Send OTP" and enter the OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number.
  4. 4From the dashboard, download e-Aadhaar, order a PVC card, or update your address/mobile number.
  5. 5Never enrolled before, or lost your mobile link? Visit your nearest Aadhaar Seva Kendra — enrolment itself needs an in-person biometric visit.

✓ PAN Card
PAN · 02

PAN Card

Apply for a free instant e-PAN using Aadhaar OTP on the Income Tax portal — or a paid physical PAN via Protean (NSDL) / UTIITSL.

View the steps
  1. 1Go to incometax.gov.in and find "Instant e-PAN" under Quick Links (needs Aadhaar linked to an active mobile number).
  2. 2Click "Get New e-PAN", enter your Aadhaar number, and accept the terms.
  3. 3Verify the OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile and confirm your e-KYC details.
  4. 4Your e-PAN (PDF with QR code) is generated in minutes — download it using your acknowledgment number.
  5. 5Need a physical card, or don't have an Aadhaar-linked mobile? Apply instead via the Protean (NSDL) or UTIITSL portal (small fee applies).

✓ DigiLocker
DGL · 03

DigiLocker

Create a free account, then fetch government-issued documents directly into your digital wallet — as legally valid as the paper original.

View the steps
  1. 1Visit digilocker.gov.in and click "Sign Up".
  2. 2Enter your mobile number, set a 6-digit security PIN, and verify with the OTP.
  3. 3Link your Aadhaar (optional, but unlocks auto-fetching) using Aadhaar OTP-based e-KYC.
  4. 4Go to "Issued Documents", search the issuing department (e.g. UIDAI, CBSE, your RTO), and select the document.
  5. 5The document is fetched into your locker instantly — share or download it any time, with a QR code for verification.

✓ Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)
AYU · 04

Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)

Check your family's eligibility and create your Ayushman Card for cashless treatment up to ₹5 lakh a year at empanelled hospitals.

View the steps
  1. 1Go to beneficiary.nha.gov.in and choose "Beneficiary" login.
  2. 2Enter your mobile number and captcha, then verify with the OTP.
  3. 3Pick your state and scheme, then search using your Aadhaar, ration card, or family ID to check eligibility.
  4. 4If eligible, complete e-KYC (Aadhaar OTP, or fingerprint at a nearby CSC/hospital) to confirm your identity.
  5. 5Download your Ayushman Card with QR code — or get it made on the spot at any empanelled hospital's Ayushman counter with your Aadhaar.

Why this site exists

Millions of citizens are entitled to services that now live only online — but the portals aren't always easy to navigate, and language is often a barrier. This is a student-built, independent awareness project that walks through the most-used government portals in plain steps, in English, Hindi, and Marathi.

We never ask for your Aadhaar, PAN, OTP, or any personal document — every "Official portal" button takes you to the real government website to complete the process yourself, safely.
